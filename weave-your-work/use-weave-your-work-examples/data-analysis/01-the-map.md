# The map

The job starts as one map: [The shop owner orders next week's stock from one list](work/map/wm-wgqqvca7r5zb.md) (`wm-wgqqvca7r5zb`). It was created `open`, with no children, and with as much body as the exercise could state before any discussion:

- A `Target` that states a state of the world — the owner orders from one list without opening the export — and a `Why` naming what the absence costs: a hand scan misses slow-moving items, and a missed item is a stockout the following week.
- An `Acceptance direction` with a specific observable result rather than the default clause: two consecutive lists in `results/`, the second produced by rerunning the written process. The default clause was not used because the map's target has its own outcome that can be checked, whether or not the children are done.
- Four `Not yet specified` entries, one of each built-in type: a decision to make (what counts as needing a reorder), a fact to establish (whether the export's SKU is the supplier's key), an assumption (the four weeks are a normal month), and a goal to clarify (one row per product or one order per supplier). Each carries its impact line, so a reader can tell what resolving it would change.

Nothing else was written. `Decisions so far` waits for the grill, and `Out of scope` was added when the exercise decided it stops at the list rather than a real supplier integration.

The acceptance direction is the part to read closely. It names the evidence (the two files), the arithmetic a reader can redo, and the columns each row carries. A review can hold it without asking the owner anything.
