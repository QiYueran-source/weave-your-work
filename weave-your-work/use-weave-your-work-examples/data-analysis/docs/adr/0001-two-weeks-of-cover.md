# Reorder below two weeks of cover

The restock list reorders a product when its on-hand stock covers less than two weeks, where cover is on-hand units divided by the average weekly sales over the four most recent weeks, and orders the difference. A per-product threshold was rejected because the owner would maintain five numbers and have to explain each one, while a uniform threshold is checked by reading one line of the process page. Two weeks rather than one because a delivery takes about a week, and the second week is the buffer that keeps a fast seller from running out before the order arrives.
