## Target

Know whether the nickel catalyst reaches the yield and purity floor on all three substrates, and have the raw data in a form the pilot decision can read.

## Why

The pilot costs a week of reactor time. Running it before the screen would spend that week on a guess.

## Acceptance direction

A screening table covers all three substrates, names the yield and purity for palladium and nickel under the same conditions, and a process chemist can trace every row to a run record. The comparison protocol is written before the runs start.

## Boundaries

- No pilot runs under this map.
- No new ligands beyond the two already in the lab.
