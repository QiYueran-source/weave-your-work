## Target

Run the 5 L pilot under the conditions the screen selected, and record whether the pilot reproduces the screening yield and purity.

## Why

The screen is small glassware. The pilot is the first run at the volume the plant would use, and scale changes mixing and heat transfer.

## Acceptance direction

A pilot record names the chosen catalyst and conditions, the charge sheet, the yield and purity at 5 L, and the deviation from the screening result. The process chemist signs the record.

## Boundaries

- No change to the catalyst or ligand after the charge sheet is signed.
- No campaign beyond one batch.
