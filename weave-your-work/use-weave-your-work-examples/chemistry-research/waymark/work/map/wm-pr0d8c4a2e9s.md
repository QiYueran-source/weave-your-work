## Target

Decide whether the production scale-up keeps palladium or waits for a nickel ligand campaign.

Why: the pilot proved palladium at 5 L, but palladium is the largest cost line; nickel is about a tenth of the catalyst cost and still misses the 70% yield floor on two substrates. The production route must be locked by 15 December.

## Acceptance direction

The decision record names palladium and the evidence: the pilot's 87% yield and 99.1% purity at 5 L, the screening table's nickel shortfall, and the 15 December route lock. A process chemist who did not join the grill can check the record against the table and the pilot record.

Review point: the ADR names palladium and the reason; the pilot record and the screening table support it; the route lock is before the supply deadline.
