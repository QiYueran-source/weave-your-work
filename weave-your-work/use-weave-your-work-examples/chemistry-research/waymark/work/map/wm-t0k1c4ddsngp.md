## Target

Decide which catalyst carries the Suzuki coupling into the 5 L pilot, with evidence a process chemist can review.

## Why

The palladium catalyst is the largest cost line in the coupling step. The lab has a nickel alternative that may reach the same yield at a tenth of the catalyst cost, but nobody has run it on the three substrates that matter.

## Acceptance direction

The map's decision record names the chosen catalyst and its conditions, cites a screening table with all three substrates and a pilot record at 5 L. A process chemist who did not run the screen can check the decision against the table and the pilot record.

## Decisions so far

- Palladium stays for the pilot; nickel misses the 70% yield floor on 4-bromotoluene (64%) and 2-bromonaphthalene (41%). Source: `lab/screening-table.csv`.
- The 5 L pilot ran under palladium at 87% yield and 99.1% purity, a -4 point deviation on 4-bromotoluene within the mixing correction. Source: `lab/pilot-record.md`.

## Boundaries

- No change to the substrate scope before the pilot.
- No supplier negotiation in this map.
