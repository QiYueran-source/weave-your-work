# Palladium for the production scale-up

The production route keeps palladium rather than waiting for a nickel ligand campaign. The nickel catalyst misses the 70% yield floor on 4-bromotoluene (64%) and 2-bromonaphthalene (41%), and the ligand that might close the gap is not in the lab; ordering it takes two weeks and would push the route lock past the supply team's 15 December date. The palladium pilot already delivered 87% yield and 99.1% purity at 5 L, so the cost premium is the price of a route that is ready now. The nickel ligand work continues in R&D, outside the production window.

## Status

Accepted.

## Considered Options

- **Wait for the nickel ligand campaign.** It could cut the catalyst cost by about 90%, but the ligand is not in the lab and the week would slip the 15 December route lock.
- **Switch to nickel without the campaign.** The screen's 64% and 41% on two substrates are below the 70% floor, so the route would not meet the pilot's benchmark.

## Consequences

Production carries the palladium cost. The nickel ligand work becomes an R&D probe, not a production dependency, and the route lock holds.
