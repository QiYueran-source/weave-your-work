# Comparison protocol

## Substrates

1. 4-bromoanisole
2. 4-bromotoluene
3. 2-bromonaphthalene

## Catalysts and conditions

- Palladium control: Pd(OAc)2, 2 mol%, XPhos, 3 equiv K2CO3, dioxane/water 4:1, 80 C, 12 h.
- Nickel: NiCl2(dme), 5 mol%, dtbbpy, 3 equiv K2CO3, dioxane/water 4:1, 80 C, 12 h.

## Sampling and analysis

Sample at 12 h. Yield by calibrated GC against dodecane; purity by HPLC area percent. The same base batch and the same GC/HPLC method are used for all six runs.

## Pass rule

Nickel passes when all three substrates reach at least 70% yield and 98% purity. Palladium is the control; a nickel run is compared with the palladium run on the same substrate.
