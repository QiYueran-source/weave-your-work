# Record Settled Details in Tickets

Every detail settled in discussion goes into the waymark ticket that owns it, as soon as it is settled. Do not wait for the end of a session or a batch of decisions.

## What to do

1. When a design question is settled in conversation, write the settled decision into the body of the map or task that owns it before asking the next question.
2. Put the detail in the section that owns it: a map's Decisions so far, a task's body, a map's Out of scope, and so on.
3. When no ticket owns the detail yet, add it to the map's Not yet specified or create the ticket that should own it.
4. Do not leave settled details only in the conversation. A later session, another person, or the skill's own flows read the tickets, not the chat.

## Why

The conversation is not durable. The tickets are.
