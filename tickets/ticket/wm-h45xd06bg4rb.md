## What to build

Assertions a review can read back against a testimony, and the example that shows the shape they check. A testimony closed with a question that has no answer, a settled line with no landing, and a testimony carrying a map or ticket field are each findable.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At least three assertions, each naming what reads it
- [ ] The assertions cover the sections the testimony defines
- [ ] The example testimony passes every assertion
