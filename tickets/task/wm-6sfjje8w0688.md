## Action

Write `weave-your-work/task-design/body.md`: the shared task content rules plus the delivery and probe section lists. Each section gets its purpose, its format, and a directly usable example. Delivery sections are Action, Acceptance signal, and Failure recovery. Probe sections are Action, Acceptance signal, Result, and Next step. Boundaries is optional for both kinds, and unused sections are omitted. Human steps inside Action carry the `Person:` prefix.

## Acceptance signal

A reader can write a delivery task and a probe task from `body.md` alone, and each section's example shows the format the section asks for.
