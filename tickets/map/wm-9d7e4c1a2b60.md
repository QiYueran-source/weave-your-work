## Target

Define the operating procedure for creating, exploring, splitting, updating, reviewing, and advancing a map without putting workflow rules into the map body reference.

Why: `body.md` defines what map content means and how it is written; the map skill also needs a separate procedure for how an owner works with that content.

## Acceptance direction

The SOP reference exists under `weave-your-work/map/`, covers the map lifecycle and its hand-offs to explore, split, and review, and a reader can use it to advance the worked example map without inventing the sequence.

All children are complete and no further children are needed.

Review point: Check that the procedure names each lifecycle operation, its owner, its hand-off, and the body sections it updates.

## Decisions so far

- The operating procedure is a sibling submap of Map body format under Map skill. It is not part of `body.md` and is not nested under the body-format map. Source: user confirmation.
- The procedure stays deferred: it is not worked on until the map body format, explore, split, and review child skills are settled. Source: user confirmation.
- A new map is discussed before it is split. When the map carries fog, that discussion is explore; when it does not, it is grill on the first layer. Split runs only after the target and the layer are settled. Source: user confirmation.
- Changing a map that has already been split is a change to its children, not a one-file edit. The branches whose meaning depends on the changed sentence are revised in the same run, and a closed child whose verdict was given against the old content is either reopened for a new review or retired and replaced. Source: user confirmation.
- The procedure points at the update skill for the three dispositions and at `frontmatter.md` for who may write what; it states only when a change reaches update and what has to be true before one starts. Source: user confirmation.
