## Target

Design the root `weave-logs/` directory for shared, unstructured records of longer research, discussion, evidence, and reasoning that support maps and tasks without becoming tickets.

Why: maps and tasks need to retain their current state and conclusions without losing the material that informed them.

## Acceptance direction

The `weave-logs/` directory and its Markdown records are defined clearly enough for teams to store and cite longer evidence without confusing records with tickets or target-tree nodes.

All children are complete and no further children are needed.

Review point: Check that the directory, file-sharing, citation, and short-versus-long record rules are explicit and keep records outside the target tree.

## Decisions so far

- Longer records live as shared Markdown files under the root `weave-logs/` directory. They are not tickets, do not use ticket frontmatter, are not processed by Waymark, and do not enter the target tree or ticket lifecycle. Source: user confirmation.
- Weave-log bodies have no required structure. Source: user confirmation.
- Short facts, observations, and evidence remain inline in a map or task body. A longer record becomes a separate weave log when it needs multiple pieces of evidence, discussion, or reasoning; will be cited by multiple tickets; or makes the ticket body hard to scan. Source: user confirmation.
- Tickets cite weave logs with ordinary Markdown relative links placed next to the relevant decision, fact, or fog entry. Source: user confirmation.
- A weave log does not need a reverse list of supporting tickets. A reverse list is optional when multiple tickets share the record or the log needs context when read alone. Source: user confirmation.
- Weave-log files use `YYYY-MM-DD_operator_topic.md`: the date, operator, and topic are separated by underscores, and the topic uses a human-readable kebab-case name. Source: user confirmation.
- This map designs the weave-log directory and citation convention; it is a map child of the Execution skills map. Source: user confirmation.
