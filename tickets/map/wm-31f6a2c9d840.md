## Target

Design testimony: the record of a grill — what a person said while a decision was being discussed, their answers, the ideas raised along the way, the alternatives that lost — kept as the discussion happens so nothing spoken is lost. A testimony holds grill discussion only: research, what an agent did during a run, and a ticket's conclusions are not part of it.

Why: a grill raises ideas faster than the tickets can hold them, and the ideas that were not chosen are the first thing a later session loses.

## Acceptance direction

The `grill/testimony.md` reference exists and settles the category, title/owner/status, link and citation rule, and scope; the grill `SKILL.md` points at it. A testimony is a waymark record item, not a target-tree node.

All children are complete and no further children are needed.

Review point: Check that the reference settles the scope, category, title/owner/status, link and citation rule, and keeps the testimony outside the target tree.

## Decisions so far

- The record is named testimony (口供), and the files live under `testimony/`. The name was chosen on 2026-11-08: it is what an interview produces (user confirmed).
- Testimony is a reference file inside grill, not a standalone skill (user confirmed on 2026-11-08). The deliverable is `weave-your-work/grill/testimony.md`; the delivery shape is recorded in [Grill skill](tickets/map/wm-7zvr8cnts3rs.md).
- A testimony holds a grill and nothing else: what the person said while a decision was discussed, including the ideas that were not taken. Research findings, run history, and a ticket's conclusions are not part of it (user confirmed, 2026-11-08). This replaces the earlier scope, "longer research, discussion, evidence, and reasoning".

A longer record becomes a separate weave log when it needs multiple pieces of evidence, discussion, or reasoning; will be cited by multiple tickets; or makes the ticket body hard to scan.~~ Superseded: length and evidence no longer decide whether a record is written, only "a grill happened" does. Where research and evidence go instead is open (see Not yet specified).
- ~~Testimonies live as shared Markdown files under the root `testimony/` directory. They are not tickets, do not use ticket frontmatter, are not processed by Waymark, and do not enter the target tree or ticket lifecycle (user confirmed).~~ Superseded on 2026-11-08: a testimony is a waymark record item, not a plain file. It is still not a work item and not a target-tree node. See below.
- ~~Testimony bodies have no required structure (user confirmed).~~ Superseded on 2026-11-08: the body has two sections, `Question tree` and `Discussion`; the content inside them is free-form.
- Tickets cite a testimony with an ordinary Markdown relative link placed next to the relevant decision, fact, or fog entry (user confirmed).
- A testimony does not need a reverse list of supporting tickets. Add an optional `Related tickets` list when several tickets share the testimony, or when it reads better with the tickets named (user confirmed).
- ~~Testimony files use `YYYY-MM-DD_operator_topic.md`: the date, operator, and topic are separated by underscores, and the topic uses a human-readable kebab-case name (user confirmed).~~ Superseded on 2026-11-08: the title is the topic; `created_at`, `creator`, and `updated_at` carry the date, operator, and last edit; the file is the waymark id.
- The testimony uses a new `testimony` category at path `testimony/` (user confirmed on 2026-11-08). It is a record item, not a work item; it does not enter the target tree. Waymark scans only the category root, so `testimony/summaries/` holds the post-hoc summaries outside the category (verified in a scratch project: subdirectories are not scanned).
- `owner` is the person the grill is for: the owner of the discussion's work item, or the user for a standalone grill (user confirmed on 2026-11-08). `creator` is the operator or agent.
- Testimony is a waymark record item (user confirmed on 2026-11-08). The title is the topic. It has waymark front matter and a status, but it is not a work item and not a target-tree node. `created_at` and `creator` carry the date and operator; `updated_at` records the last edit; `owner` names the accountable party of the discussion.
- The testimony body has two sections: `Question tree`, a short checkbox outline rewritten at the end of each round, and `Discussion`, what was said, appended at the end of each round. The run writes the testimony once per round, not after every answer (user confirmed on 2026-11-08).
- When a question becomes work, the testimony records `work: <what>` and the caller creates the map or task in the target tree. The testimony does not create or track work items (user confirmed on 2026-11-08).
- Three acts, three names: `refine` splits a broad question into narrower questions in the question tree; `hand off` moves a settled question to the target tree as work; `split` turns a map's next layer into child maps or tasks. The grill uses the first two; the split skill owns the third (user confirmed on 2026-11-08).
- Multiple features are parallel siblings: sibling questions in the question tree, sibling child maps or tasks in the target tree. They are not a chain; order is a dependency edge between maps, and tasks carry no edge (user confirmed on 2026-11-08).
- Status is `open` while the grill runs and `closed` when it ends; tickets cite a testimony only after it closes (user confirmed on 2026-11-08).
- Work queries use `ready --category map --category task`; a testimony is a record item and is not part of the work frontier (user confirmed on 2026-11-08).
