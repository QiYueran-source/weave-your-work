## Target

Design a `log` ticket category for recording longer research, discussion, evidence, and reasoning that does not fit in a map or task body. Log ticket bodies are unstructured; the category does not participate in target-tree refinement or completion.

Why: maps and tasks need to retain their current state and conclusions without losing the material that informed them.

## Acceptance direction

The log ticket category and its relationship to map and task tickets are defined clearly enough to use without confusing log records with target-tree nodes.

All children are complete and no further children are needed.

Review point: Check that the category, attachment relation, lifecycle, and body-format expectations are explicit and consistent with the target tree.

## Decisions so far

- `log` is a new ticket category. Source: user confirmation.
- Log tickets may be attached to map or task tickets through a separate relation, not the target-tree parent-child relation. A log does not need a target-tree parent; the attachment relation is its only work-item association. Source: user confirmation.
- A log may be attached to a map or task, but not to another log. Source: user confirmation.
- Log tickets retain ordinary ticket identity and a status field, while their body has no required structure. Closing a log means its recorded discussion or investigation is confirmed; log status is not the ordinary map/task lifecycle. Source: user confirmation.
- This map designs the log ticket category; it is a map child of the Execution skills map. Source: user confirmation.
