## Target

Design the record of a grill: what a person said while a decision was being discussed — their answers, the ideas raised along the way, the alternatives that lost — kept as the discussion happens so nothing spoken is lost. A record holds grill discussion only: research, what an agent did during a run, and a ticket's conclusions are not part of it.

Why: a grill raises ideas faster than the tickets can hold them, and the ideas that were not chosen are the first thing a later session loses.

## Acceptance direction

The record's name, directory, file naming, and citation rule are settled, and the record is clearly not a ticket or a target-tree node. A grill leaves one record that the tickets it served can link.

All children are complete and no further children are needed.

Review point: Check that the scope, name, directory, file naming, and citation rule are settled and keep the record outside the target tree.

## Decisions so far

- A record holds a grill and nothing else: what the person said while a decision was discussed, including the ideas that were not taken. Research findings, run history, and a ticket's conclusions are not part of it (user confirmed, 2026-11-08). This replaces the earlier scope, "longer research, discussion, evidence, and reasoning".
- ~~Short facts, observations, and evidence remain inline in a map or task body. A longer record becomes a separate weave log when it needs multiple pieces of evidence, discussion, or reasoning; will be cited by multiple tickets; or makes the ticket body hard to scan.~~ Superseded: length and evidence no longer decide whether a record is written, only "a grill happened" does. Where research and evidence go instead is open (see Not yet specified).
- Records live as shared Markdown files under the root `weave-logs/` directory. They are not tickets, do not use ticket frontmatter, are not processed by Waymark, and do not enter the target tree or ticket lifecycle (user confirmed). The directory name follows the record's name and is open.
- Record bodies have no required structure (user confirmed).
- Tickets cite records with ordinary Markdown relative links placed next to the relevant decision, fact, or fog entry (user confirmed).
- A record does not need a reverse list of supporting tickets. Add an optional `Related tickets` list when several tickets share the record, or when the record reads better with the tickets named (user confirmed).
- Record files use `YYYY-MM-DD_operator_topic.md`: the date, operator, and topic are separated by underscores, and the topic uses a human-readable kebab-case name (user confirmed).

## Not yet specified

## Out of scope
