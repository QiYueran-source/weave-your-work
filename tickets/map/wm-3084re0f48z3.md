## Target

Design the explore skill: clear the fog of a map. Its one responsibility is to take the map's Not yet specified items and resolve them until the section is empty.

Why: without explore, a map can carry fog forever and never move; with it, every fog item either becomes a decision, a child, an out-of-scope item, or is dropped.

## Acceptance direction

All children are complete and no further children are needed: the SKILL.md exists under `weave-your-work/explore/`, and a sample map with fog can be cleared end to end, with each fog item resolved or dropped and the Not yet specified section empty.

## Decisions so far

- explore reads the map body instead of inventing its own fog classes: the entry types come from the map body format (Assumption, Fact to establish, Decision to make, Goal to clarify, plus custom types), and explore does not rewrite them. Source: user confirmation.
- explore designs one clearing flow per fog type. Source: user confirmation.
- explore references grill for the discussion part of clearing; grill references the weave-log records so a discussion lands in a record as it happens. Source: user confirmation.
- Landing split: explore owns the map decisions and fog-clearing landings (Decisions so far, updated Target, struck-through fog, child hand-off to split); grill owns the term, weave-log, and ADR landings. explore references grill for discussion; grill does not clear fog. Source: user confirmation.
- Common clearing skeleton: judge, act, land, strike through. Each fog entry is first judged for how concrete it is; if it is not concrete enough, act (research or discussion through grill); when concrete, land it into the map (Decisions so far, Target, Out of scope, or hand to split); then strike the entry through in Not yet specified. Source: user confirmation.
- Fact to establish flow: agent researches first, locally and online, with the result carrying a source; if neither place yields a reliable answer, the item goes to a person — first the owner through grill's question protocol, and when the owner does not know, the owner is reminded to ask others through an issue. Landed facts go into Decisions so far with their source, and the fog entry is struck through. explore records facts honestly and does not decide whether a fact is also a term; grill decides whether something lands in GLOSSARY. Source: user confirmation.
- Assumption flow: an assumption is first researched for support like a fact, but confirmation usually cannot come from a fact at all (for example "adding a button makes people xxx, so that xxx"); such an assumption can only be supported. If no hard support exists (for example it is only a particular opinion), a probe task is recommended to test it, but it is not mandatory: to save time the assumption may be treated as reasonable in most simple cases, and a probe is strongly suggested when the impact is large. When supported, the assumption lands in Decisions so far with its evidence; when refuted, the affected Target, Acceptance direction, Out of scope, or new fog is updated instead, recording the counter-evidence, and the fog entry is struck through. Source: user confirmation.
- Decision to make flow: when the owner cannot decide because information is missing, the decision is not forced; it is split into an Assumption fog entry or a probe task instead. A landed decision goes into Decisions so far and the fog entry is struck through; explore only marks whether the decision can be split into a child, and split does the actual child creation later — explore never splits. Source: user confirmation.
- Goal to clarify flow: the goal is clarified with the owner through grill, then the Target section is updated; when the clarification changes the acceptance standard, the Acceptance direction section is updated together with Target. The fog entry is struck through. Source: user confirmation.
