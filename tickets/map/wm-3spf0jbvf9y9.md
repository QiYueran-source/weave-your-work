## Target

Design the review skill: when a map reaches reviewing (all children complete), check the result against the acceptance direction and review point agreed before, and decide one of four verdicts: passed (closed), needs changes (return to delivering or planned), needs discussion (call grill), or wrong direction (failed, closed with outcome=failed and a replacement sibling).

Why: a map closes only when a reviewer confirms it met its acceptance direction.

## Acceptance direction

The skill exists under `weave-your-work/review/`: SKILL.md states who reviews, how the acceptance direction is checked, and the four verdicts; a sample map is reviewed.

All children are complete and no further children are needed.

Review point: Check that the SKILL.md names who reviews, how the acceptance direction is checked, and the four verdicts, and that a sample map walks through a review.

## Decisions so far

- Review owns reviewing to closed or return; the map skill owns delivering to reviewing (user confirmed).
- Anyone may review, including the owner reviewing their own work; the earlier "non-executor" rule is dropped (user confirmed).
- Review checks along two axes, independently and reported separately: the goal check reads the map's acceptance direction and review point (did the map achieve what it promised), and the convention check reads `body.md` and the split rules (was the map built according to the format and the child rules). The two axes are not merged into one overall impression, so a failing axis is not hidden by a passing one. Every finding cites its source (the acceptance direction line or the body.md rule). Source: user confirmation.
- The checklist is not opened before review and not generated by review: the goal check reads the map's own acceptance direction and review point, the convention check reads the fixed rules in `body.md` and the split skill. A review point that cannot be checked means the goal itself is not clear; that is a fog for explore, not a reason to open a checklist. Source: user confirmation.
- Verdict has four outcomes: passed (closed), needs changes (return: not closed, back to delivering or planned, the gap recorded in the map's Decisions so far, owner fixes and is reviewed again), needs discussion (call grill to talk with the owner when signals or standards are unclear), and wrong direction (failed: closed with outcome=failed and a replacement sibling, per the execution map). Source: user confirmation.
- The report shape follows the code-review pattern without software terms: conclusion, per-axis finding list with sources, what is done well, verdict. Source: user confirmation.
